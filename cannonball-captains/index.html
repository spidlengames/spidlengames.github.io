How to install

  1. Download the file on your Android phone or tablet. If your browser asks whether to keep it, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open it and tap Install. If Android says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.
  3. Google Play Protect may call it "unrecognized". That's normal for apps that aren't from the Play Store. Choose Scan app, or More details → Install anyway.
  4. Open Cannonball Captains, make a captain, and set sail!

Amazon Fire tablets: allow installing under Settings → Security & Privacy → Apps from Unknown Sources for the app you downloaded with. Updating: install the new file over the old one. Your captains and wins are kept.

How to play

Place your fleet. Every captain has 5 ships: a Carrier (5 squares), a Battleship (4), a Destroyer (3), a Submarine (3) and a Patrol Boat (2). Drag a ship to move it and tap it to turn it. Ships can't touch each other, not even at the corners.

Take turns firing. Tap a square in Enemy Waters to fire a cannonball. A splash means a miss and passes the turn. Flames mean a hit, and you fire again!

Sink them all. Hit every square of a ship to sink it. The first captain to sink the whole enemy fleet wins.
