How to join the test

  1. Join the tester group. Open the Cannonball Captains testers group, sign in with the Google account you use on your Android device, and click Join group.
  2. Become a tester. Open the testing page with the same account and click Become a tester. If it says the app isn't available yet, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.
  3. Install it from Google Play. Tap the Google Play link on that page, or open Cannonball Captains on Google Play on your Android device, and tap Install.
  4. Please keep it installed for at least 14 days and play now and then. Google asks for this before a new game can go public. Thank you!

Installed it from this website before? The Google Play version installs over it, and your captains and wins are kept. Amazon Fire tablets don't have Google Play, so please play in your browser for now.

How to play

Place your fleet. Every captain has 5 ships: a Carrier (5 squares), a Battleship (4), a Destroyer (3), a Submarine (3) and a Patrol Boat (2). Drag a ship to move it and tap it to turn it. Ships can't touch each other, not even at the corners.

Take turns firing. Tap a square in Enemy Waters to fire a cannonball. A splash means a miss and passes the turn. Flames mean a hit, and you fire again!

Sink them all. Hit every square of a ship to sink it. The first captain to sink the whole enemy fleet wins.

Treasure and bombs. With treasure on, chests hide in the sea. Fire at one to earn a bomb, then pull back the slingshot to drop it on a whole group of squares. A bomb ends your turn.
