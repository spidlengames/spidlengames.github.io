Privacy policy

Cannonball Captains, by Spidlen Games · Effective October 10, 2026

The short version: Cannonball Captains has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts. Your captains stay on your device. Online games only share what the other player needs to see: your captain's name and character, and your moves.

What stays on your device

  • Captains: their names, chosen characters, any picture you upload as an avatar, and their wins and losses.
  • Settings: sound and vibration on or off, and the language.
  • An unfinished online game, so you can continue it, and a list of your recent online game codes, so old games can be cleaned up.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else, except as described under online games below. Avatars you upload never leave your device: other players see one of the built-in characters instead. Uninstalling the game deletes all of this.

Online games

Playing with a friend online uses Google Firebase (Realtime Database and anonymous Authentication), a service run by Google. When you play online:

  • The game signs in anonymously: Firebase gives your copy of the game a random ID. No name, email address or phone number is involved.
  • For each game, Firebase stores: the 4-letter game code; the two players' random IDs; each captain's name and built-in character; which version of the game they use; when each player was last connected; the shots fired and their results; and, at the end, each player's ship positions and a code that proves the fleet didn't change during the game.
  • Only the two players in a game can read it. Your ship positions stay on your device until the game is over.
  • As with any internet service, Google receives your device's IP address when the game connects. See Firebase's privacy information. The database is located in the United States.

How long it's kept: a game is deleted when it's over and its creator leaves, or when the last player leaves. Your device also deletes your games that are more than a day old the next time you open the game. A game that both players abandon may stay stored until one of them opens the game again, or until we remove it.

The captain name you choose is shown to the friend you play with, so it's a good idea to use a nickname or first name only.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. The game asks only for internet access (for online games) and vibration.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Children

Cannonball Captains is made for families. It doesn't ask for anyone's age, real name, email address or any other contact details, and it has no chat. Players see each other only through a captain name and a built-in character.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement. The site uses no cookies and no analytics.
