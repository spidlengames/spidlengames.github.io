Maze of the Bobcats

A maze chase starring Bobbie the Bobcat, made for the arcade cabinet at Bellview Elementary in Ashland, Oregon. Eat the dots, grab Bobcat Tickets, and stay one step ahead of the teachers!

See the mazes How to play

Made for Bellview Elementary Arcade cabinet · Joystick and buttons

Go, Bobcats!

Six school mazes

Morning Bell, Math Class, the Library, the Reflection Form, Lunch & Recess and Homework Time, each one drawn like its own corner of school.

A twist in every maze

A tardy bell that sends the teachers back to the lounge, numbers to eat in order, quiet zones where the teachers can't see you, banana peels and more.

Bobcat Tickets

Grab a Bobcat Ticket and the teachers run for the lounge. Catch them on the way for big points!

Real teachers, as friendly ghosts

The school can add its own teachers as cartoon ghosts, each with a funny line when they catch you. Their pictures stay on the cabinet and are never put online.

School power-ups

A Hall Pass to slip past a teacher, a Recess Bell that freezes them, speedy Sneakers, and Pizza Friday, when every dot is worth double.

A Sticker Book

15 stickers to collect for fun things, like clearing a level without getting caught. Type your name at the end of a game to keep them.

Leaderboards

The best scores of today, this week and all time, so there's a new champion every day.

One or two players

Play alone, or with a friend: take turns with your own mazes, play as a team in the same maze, or play together at the same time, each with your own joystick.

Made for an arcade cabinet

A joystick and big arcade buttons, five difficulty levels from Recess to Principal's Office, and a demo that plays when nobody is around.

How to play

Eat every dot. Steer Bobbie with the joystick (or swipe on the screen) and eat all the dots to clear the maze. Bonus treats pop up now and then for extra points.

Watch out for the teachers. If a teacher catches Bobbie, you lose a life. Each teacher has their own way of chasing you, and they get faster and smarter as you go.

Turn the tables. Eat a Bobcat Ticket in a corner and the teachers run back to the lounge. Catch them on the way, and each one in a row is worth more.

Clear all six mazes and start round 2, a little faster. How far can you get?

Maze of the Bobcats was made just for Bellview Elementary's arcade cabinet, so it isn't available to download. The game is in English.
